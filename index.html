How It Works

From data to decision in milliseconds.

A five-layer pipeline that handles everything — so you focus on strategy, not execution mechanics.

1

Data Ingestion

Multi-source price feeds via broker aggregation layer with SBBO logic. Economic calendar events, macro news, and social sentiment pulled in real-time. Anomaly detection flags bad feeds before they contaminate signals.

2

Signal Processing

C++ core computes EMA crossovers, ATR trends, RSI levels, Bollinger position, and Z-score in a single composite score per symbol. Adaptive thresholds self-adjust based on recent market regime.

3

Risk Gating

Before any signal reaches execution, the risk engine validates daily drawdown headroom, available position slots, event proximity, and Kelly-optimal sizing. Non-compliant signals are rejected, not scaled.

4

Decision Output

Qualified signals are surfaced as structured outputs — direction, size, stop, take-profit — ready for your review or auto-execution via broker adapters (IBKR, FIX protocol).

5

Observability

Every decision, every metric, every anomaly logged to Prometheus and visualized in Grafana. The system knows its own health — and so do you, in real time.
